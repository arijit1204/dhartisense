Field plan

Plan your field

Drag the 💧 pin to your well, pump or canal outlet. The pipe layout follows it.

A starting layout from your field shape, typical spacings for the crop and standard sprinkler and drip dimensions. It does not know your slope, soil, trees, wells or buildings, so adjust it on the ground. Yellow line: your boundary. Dashed tan: headland for turning the tractor. Tan lanes: working paths. Green lines: crop rows. Blue: water lines.
